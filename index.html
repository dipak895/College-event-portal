About the Portal

The College Event Management Portal helps students stay informed about everything happening on campus. Instead of searching notice boards and group chats, students can browse events, search notices and submit their registration from one website.

The portal is built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Bootstrap 5 and works on desktop, tablet and mobile screens.
